ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the second arcana is the High Priestess, embodying true, soft power, secret knowledge, wisdom and spiritual development. This is a female archetype that leaves its mark even if it is in the Matrix of men. For this energy, stature is characteristic, which manifests itself in a special attitude toward oneself. Such people love and understand themselves, walking with their heads held high.
For the owner of the second energy, unity, unification, peace and good are important. This is the energy of harmony - it can harmonize itself, people, space. Through meditations and other practices it can bring itself and others into a state of calm. Reaching a state of balance and harmony is possible not only thanks to meditation and yoga, but also with the help of creative processes, such as creating music, works of art, jewelry, and also creating a cozy atmosphere in the house.
The second energy has an esoteric nature. Its owners are sensitive, calm, wise and deep. They can sense the thoughts of other people, feel tension or joy "in the air". Thanks to a thin perception of the world, they can know how they should or should not act.
Often possess oratory art, love to support conversations on various topics with different people.
Many negative manifestations of this energy flow from excessive sensitivity: such people can be easily offended, they can be capricious, prone to addictions. People possessing the second energy are capable of sensing the energy vibrations of other people, which often leads to a sense of discomfort and causes distrust. Duality, characteristic of the energy, can become a cause of doubt and inconstancy, which prevents establishment of a connection with the flow. In addition, such negative manifestations as double-facedness, falseness and secretiveness are a consequence of duality and can significantly affect the lives and relationships of people.
It is important for representatives of the second energy to spend time in nature to periodically ground themselves, study different kinds of information, adhere to proper nutrition and monitor health.

PROBLEMS WITH PARENTS
The zone of parent-child relationships is located at points a, a2, a1 and it is responsible for 2 directions: relationships with your parents and relationships with your children. Energies of the PCR zone show why you came to your parents / your children came to you and what problems can be in your relationships.
Being a child, it was perhaps difficult for you to understand when parents behaved sincerely and when they hid something. It is possible that there were double standards in the family: what was forbidden for children was allowed for parents. You tried to adapt to these realities, learned to be a good child for parents, suppressing your real emotions and feelings.
You need to learn to trust and listen to your inner voice. Do not refuse help from loved ones if you need it. Talk openly about your feelings. If someone hurt or offended you, do not be afraid to tactfully say so to the person.
RELATIONSHIPS WITH CHILDREN
You need to become a model for your child. If you want to teach him something, then first show it by your own example.
Talk honestly and openly, do not hide or keep silent about anything. Explain to the child everything as it is: why you feel this way, what emotions you experience and from what. Do not keep emotions in yourself, but also do not behave aggressively toward the child. It is important to teach the child to trust you so that he can open up and talk about his feelings and problems. Only then will you be able to create a harmonious and kind atmosphere in the family, where each family member will feel comfortable and protected.
