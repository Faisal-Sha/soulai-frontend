ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the first arcana is the Magician.
This energy makes a person focused, capable of immersing themselves in work and creative processes. Such people can be closed, slightly detached from the world, being inside themselves for a long time. Secret knowledge, esotericism attract people with the first energy. Often such people possess extrasensory abilities and well-developed sensitivity.
People with the first energy are good masters who are capable of creating magic, translating ideas from the spiritual world into matter and thus creating something new. They can easily connect to the field of magic and draw ideas for realization from there. It is important not to leave your thoughts in your head, but to translate them into matter in any form.
Owners of the first arcana feel a desire for movement and adventurism, they value independence and tend to follow their own rules. They are endowed with exceptional intelligence, a deep understanding and vision of the world. They can transfer knowledge and experience, taking the position of a mentor or "guru".
Egoism is an integral part of this energy. It is important to be aware of the presence of this quality and not be afraid of it, but, on the contrary, use it for the benefit of promoting and implementing your ideas and plans. The energy of the first arcana endows a person with leadership qualities that may be necessary to implement his ideas in life. However, due to the closed nature of this energy, leadership will most likely manifest itself covertly ("grey cardinal"). If it is necessary to promote their projects, people with the first energy can be skillful speakers, capable of carrying their thoughts and ideas to the masses.
Negative manifestations, as a rule, arise with a strongly overstated or understated self-esteem. They can be expressed in self-doubt, fear of theft of ideas, not bringing their ideas to realization. In the case of an excessively inflated ego, people with the first energy can be arrogant, not perceiving the opinions and needs of others.
Cunning, self-interest, vindictiveness and manipulation are characteristic negative manifestations of the first energy. They can keep grudges in their memory for a long time, which can lead to an unexpected manifestation of revenge in the future.

PROBLEMS WITH PARENTS
The zone of parent-child relationships is located at points a, a2, a1 and it is responsible for 2 directions: relationships with your parents and relationships with your children. Energies of the PCR zone show why you came to your parents / your children came to you and what problems can be in your relationships.
It is possible that in childhood your talents were ignored and you were not noticed. Parents behaved selfishly and cared first of all about their own desires, pushing you to the background. Being a child, you fought for their attention in the hope of justifying expectations and getting praise. Also, if you have brothers or sisters, then, probably, you had to compete with them for the attention of parents. You need to heal from childhood traumas to move on. Accept parents as they are. Forgive them and do not keep evil in your heart. Care for them with all love and gratitude for the experience gained. Value and respect your space, do not be afraid to show yourself to the world, realize talents through creativity. You no longer need to compete with someone. Enjoy your life and create unique, as only you know how.
RELATIONSHIPS WITH CHILDREN
To build close and trusting relationships with a child, learn to feel his desires and correctly understand emotions. Do not succumb to manipulation, but also do not become an authoritarian parent. Communicate as equals, be open, share thoughts. Do not devalue the child's ideas, even if they seem frivolous to you. Support him and give the opportunity to try his hand in different directions. Talk about your feelings openly and do not keep anything in yourself.
