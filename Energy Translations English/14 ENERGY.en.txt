ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
Archetype of fourteenth energy — artist and creator. This is very creative, refined, soft energy. Lightness, spirituality and connection with higher, connection with flow is inherent to it. For representatives of this energy it's not important how they look from point of view of assessment by society, it's important for them to be in state of inspiration and have possibility to manifest themselves. Creative structure of this energy endows person with tendency to frequent mood swings.
First orientation of this energy — creativity, creation of works of art. Such people easily succeed in connection to flow and reading information, which they easily transform into art (into singing, music, creation of paintings or sculptures etc.). Second direction — spirituality. They often feel desire to find their place in world and their true path. Turn to meditations, to reading spiritual literature, as well as to other ways of searching self, what allows them to strengthen connection with higher «Self» and find their place in Universe. This leads to greater self-giving, empathy and sensitivity, as well as to development of deeper understanding of meaning of life.
People with fourteenth energy are calm and peaceful. They are intellectuals, connoisseurs of beautiful, refined lovers of art. They are endowed with wisdom and morality. This is decent, wise and creative soul. They understand and accept life, love to study many things, including foreign languages and cultures. People who possess this energy can use it for healing. Help people heal their wounds.
Creative structure endows owners of fourteenth energy with excessive sensitivity, what can lead to such minus manifestations as vulnerability, capriciousness and touchiness. They can manifest extremes in their behavior, often stay in state of emotional swings. Second direction of minuses — closedness, leading to rigidity, callousness and harshness. Attachment to material can make person greedy and in such way block creative flow.
