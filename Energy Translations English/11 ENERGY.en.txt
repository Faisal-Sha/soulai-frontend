General description of energy
Archetype of the eleventh energy — "Strength" (masculine energy). At plus manifestation, a person with the eleventh energy is endowed with physical and/or internal strength. This energy gives the person a strong internal core.

They love to be in first place, can lead people behind them, in which bright charisma helps them.
Such people know how to solve problems and take responsibility on themselves, they are persistent and ambitious, can move forward despite anything. They value practicality and adaptability, with ease know how to build processes maximally effectively for reaching the result. They manifest curiosity and desire to learn new. People with this energy can encounter problems related with relationships with mother. Most often this will go from childhood, where the parent suppressed their child, his potential. In such cases it's important to learn to establish boundaries and express one's needs and desires, not fearing to stand up for self.
Non-acceptance of one's body and one's sexuality can lead to problems in relationships. Inability to pull self together can lead to absence of motivation and difficulties in reaching goals. In such cases it's useful to learn to manage one's emotions, establish clear goals and find support in surrounding people.
