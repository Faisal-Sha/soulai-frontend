ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
Archetype of this energy - startupper, player, inspirer. This is the only energy of the matrix that symbolizes luck. In presence of this energy in matrix it is necessary to pay attention to it in first place, since at minus manifestation it will lead person into heaviness. On other hand, at manifestedness in plus, lightness and luck will accompany person in all his undertakings.
For tenth energy lightness and joy is important, as well as necessity of constant movement. People possessing given energy are able to easily get inspired by idea, drawing those around behind self and inspiring them for actions. Due to one's lightness and shiftlessness can without problems throw self into new matters, not having clear understanding about how to reach goal. However, as soon as they start movement forward, circumstances start to build around them in such way that resources for realization of their ideas appear.
To people with tenth energy it's necessary to stay in state of joy and fun to feel lightness of life and give possibility to circumstances to build in optimal for them order. At absence of inspiration, energy will go into minus, heaviness will appear, failures will be attracted. Life of person with tenth energy can be characterized as "roller coaster" - they easily can catch luck by tail and fly up, attracting resources and opportunities, but at appearance of doubts and fears also easily can fall down. It is contraindicated to go into stagnation, immerse into self - if heavy period in life comes, it is important to start at least some movement (elementarily coming out of house and taking walk on street will help activate energy).
Representatives of this energy easily ignite, however also fast can lose enthusiasm, especially in presence of monotonous routine. This energy is also linked with passive leadership, and in case of necessity, people with this energy easily can take leading position, although they have no striving for this. Thanks to absence of egoism, they are ready to work on secondary roles. For them atmosphere, fun and state of game are important. They love adventurism and can take part in different projects if feel inspiration there. People with this energy know how to earn money, but for them main motivator is ideality of process. They are industrious and don't succumb to negative emotions when something goes not how planned.
However, people with tenth energy can see opportunities and cunning schemes, therefore this energy is widespread among scammers. It is necessary to refuse from temptation of getting easy and dishonest money, since there is chance to lose even larger sum in future.
In minus this energy is subject to fears, life without inspiration. Person becomes heavy and apathetic. Prone to worries, whims and inconsistency - takes for everything in a row, has harmful habits.
