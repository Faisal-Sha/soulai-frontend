ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the fifth arcana is the Hierophant, Priest (male energy). This archetype imposes a certain perception of oneself, when a person feels higher than the rest. These are people of high intellect, they understand more than others, and are ready to teach and give advice when necessary. In a positive manifestation, people possessing the fifth energy are characterized by calm, poise, openness and smiling nature. They value traditions and rituals, their energy is filled with conservative values.
Representatives of the fifth energy are open to various knowledge, religions and movements. It's interesting for them to learn and discover everything new and unknown. They are always moving forward toward knowledge of self and the higher. They are good and diligent students, capable of ordering the received information both internally in their head and externally with the help of various systems (tables, charts, notes, etc.). Order is important to them everywhere and in everything. Such people are not inclined to leadership, but if they find themselves in this role, they can show their leadership qualities and inspire others to achieve common goals. They possess deep understanding of processes and can effectively use their knowledge to achieve team goals.
People with the fifth energy, thanks to their high level of knowledge and understanding of the world, can become excellent teachers and mentors for those around them. Their ability to give advice, support and guide others can be indispensable in education and development of people. This archetype disposes to teaching others, to help in knowing oneself and the surrounding world. Thanks to the skill of oratory (eloquently presenting one's thoughts in oral and written form), people with the fifth energy can share their knowledge both in individual mode and to a wide public.
Excessive intellectuality of this energy can breed fear of insufficiency of knowledge and self-confidence in the fact that they already know everything necessary, which can lead to missing the opportunity for further development and learning new knowledge and experience. On the other hand - in the minus of this energy, a person can be too arrogant and so self-confident in their knowledge, rules and principles, that they become closed to other points of view and to accepting new information.
A person with the fifth energy can lean toward imposing their ideas, beliefs and methods on other people, which can cause irritation and rejection in them. The desire to teach and tell how it's right is another one of the minuses of this energy.
People with the fifth energy relate to family ties with special attention and veneration, considering them an important part of their life. For them, family life and relationships within the family embody correctness and traditions, which they value and strive to preserve. Family will have a direct influence on the life and events of this archetype.

Fifth arcana symbolizes energy of family. Its presence in the soul task points at the importance of family ties, as well as the necessity of learning and openness for new knowledge. It is important to establish harmonious relationships within your family, maintain connection with parents, partner and children. One shouldn't plant one's principles on loved ones, even if it seems that you are absolutely right and know what's better for them. It is important to realize that each of them lives their own life and has their own experience.
For the energy of the fifth arcana, it is important to go through one's own path of development and accumulate information and life experience. Precisely on the basis of this experience continue to move forward, strive for truth, share knowledge and develop your authority. However, it is important not to get fixated and not to dive too much into the world of knowledge, but to find a balance between knowledge and practical application of your knowledge for solving real situations.
Instead of imposing your lifestyle, it's important to present to those around opportunity to independently search for truth and form their individuality. Disdainful treatment of such people not only insults them, but also prevents your own development. One should understand that life continues and changes, and what seemed relevant earlier may today lose its sense. Strive to become more flexible in your thinking. MAIN WORK-THROUGH
Main work-through. Energy through which the main work-through and problems go in a person's life. We, as if, stumble through life over this energy. From birth, this energy is manifested in minus in a person.
People with the fifth energy in the work-through zone often manifest arrogance and pride, considering their opinion the only correct one and rejecting ideas and suggestions of others. Such stubbornness and unwillingness to go for compromises can lead to conflicts and breaks in relationships with those around.
For development of a healthier relationships and communication with people, it is important to learn to be more open to opinions of others and ready for dialogue and compromises. Understanding that no one is ideal, and ability to recognize one's mistakes, is a sign of maturity and ability to extract lessons from life experience.
Another manifestation of fifth energy in the work-through zone is rebellion and disagreement with established norms and rules. People possessing this energy may strive for independence and freedom of thought and actions, sometimes even manifesting stubbornness and unwillingness to acknowledge authorities and leadership. Such behavior can cause conflicts in the team and create problems in interaction with other people.
For development of the constructive side it is important to learn to use rebellion considering social norms and values. Finding harmony between one's individuality and ability to cooperate with others, respecting their beliefs and needs, contributes to reducing potential conflicts. Fifth energy can also lead to constant doubts in own abilities. People with this energy can underestimate themselves and their achievements, often thinking that there will always be someone better. They may strive for constant learning and accumulation of knowledge to reach confidence and recognition, but not use them in practice.
To overcome the feeling of uncertainty in yourself, it is necessary to actively work on strengthening your self-esteem and confidence in your knowledge and skills.
