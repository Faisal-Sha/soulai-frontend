ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The eighth energy, by its nature, doesn't yield to rigid definition of archetype. To the greatest degree it's corresponded by description "Balinese esotericist". Owners of the eighth energy can convey to those around state of harmony and pacification, however also they easily can slide into aggression and destruction. This energy can be considered the most dual in the matrix: it has two absolutely opposite sides - light (calm) and dark (destructive). Positive side of the eighth energy manifests in its ability to give those around feeling of calmness, balance and depth. However, in negative variant, it can manifest in cruelty and revengefulness.
For people with the eighth energy an important aspect is searching for balance within self, what prompts them to practices of meditation and other methods capable of helping them reach this harmony. They can also exert influence on those around, helping find balance in their lives.
Eighth energy - energy of justice. For people with this energy characteristic is striving for honesty and justice, they can feel deep dissatisfaction and suffering when injustice happens regarding them or their loved ones. It is important to learn to achieve justice through acceptance and calmness, and not through struggle and conflicts.
Logic and depth of thinking - yet another distinctive trait of these people. They are prone to rational approach and to analysis, know how to sort out cause-and-effect links, love to learn. Thanks to their insightfulness representatives of the eighth energy can see logic of events and understand what exactly leads to success or failure. They also can easily decompose any information "on shelves" and convey it to other people.
Eighth energy endows person with reliability and responsibility, one can rely on him, he will be loyal and never will betray. People with eight possess open nature and usually easily find general language with other people. Their communicability and talkativeness make them excellent interlocutors and public leaders.
From other side, in minus manifestation, people with eights can become aggressive and revengeful, they can manipulate those around in their goals. Excessive categoricalness can lead to arguments and conflicts, when eights refuse to accept opinion of another person. Lie, deception and dishonesty - also are manifestation of minuses of this energy.
