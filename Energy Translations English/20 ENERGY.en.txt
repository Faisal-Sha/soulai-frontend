ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
This energy is one of the most complex to understand. The twentieth energy lacks a clear structure and archetype. According to the classical Tarot, this is the "Judgment" arcana, which hints that a person with this energy can be just, knows how to judge and condemn. The energy of the twentieth arcana is associated with the concept of connection, as its goal is to combine different aspects, both spiritual and physical, as well as to unite people among themselves. Owners of this energy are capable of creating integral products from different components (often manifested in producers, marketers). They can also help other people find integrity in their lives.
This is an esoteric energy with developed intuition, which can connect with a flow and pass it through self, expressing what is seen in creativity or a product. It is important for people with the twentieth energy to be in a state of creative inspiration and manifest their creativity. They can turn to deep esoteric knowledge and research that helps to reveal new levels of consciousness and spirituality.
People with the twentieth energy have a close connection with their ancestry and are capable of connecting families, helping to heal ancestral problems and programs. They strive to achieve integrity in various aspects of life.
In a positive manifestation, people with the twentieth energy are stable, systemic, and authoritative. They have an internal core that helps them be composed. Such people can be managers, as they see how to unite people to make an integral product, however, they do not strive for leadership.
Directions of minuses of the twentieth energy are an absence of integrity within self or excessive rigidity and demandingness. When manifesting in minus, a representative of this energy is as if fragmented into pieces, unable to assemble themselves. Bad relationships within the family and/or ancestry are possible, which leads a person to a lack of understanding of self and an absence of internal support.
The reverse side of this sensitive energy can endow a person with negative qualities, then they become resentful, judgmental, and categorical.
