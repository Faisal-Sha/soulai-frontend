The second manifestation of minuses is "fading of the sun." This can lead to apathy and illnesses due to lack of energy. In the absence of a clear vector of direction, a person may have chaoticness in life and in actions.
People with the nineteenth energy may face problems in relationships with their father, who may be strong and powerful, and try to suppress them, literally "extinguishing their light." In addition, conflicts can arise between two strong personalities.
