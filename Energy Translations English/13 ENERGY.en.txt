ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
Thirteenth energy doesn't have a specific archetype, it is structureless. This is energy of renewal and transformation. In classic Tarot energy is represented by arcana «Death», but it's important to understand that in Matrix of Abundance death is a designation of transformation, getting rid of old and birth of new. «Daring hero» — description mostly suitable for archetype of thirteenth energy.
People with thirteenth energy possess ability to discard old and obsolete, to create place for new. They with enthusiasm relate to transformation, alterations and changes often become driving force in creation of changes. Not afraid to risk and cross boundaries to reach their goals. Help other people overcome established norms and expectations, what in final result leads to new opportunities and innovations.
Energy can manifest as creative abilities, but not creative ones, but through destruction (for example, restoration of objects). People with thirteenth energy see systems and projects well which are already no longer viable, however not always strive to creation of something new on this place. Essence and strength of this energy — destruction, but destruction for good (what needs to be destroyed so that it becomes better).
Thirteenth energy endows person with sufficiently straightforward thinking, what can lead to excessive daring. Such people are characterized by bravery, love for life and activity. Passion for changes can inspire them for new heights. Constant changes in life are integral part of life of people with this energy. Striving for transformations endows people with unpredictability, they can constantly change vector of direction of their life and activity. Besides, people with thirteenth energy can direct their vision on transformation of people, projects and companies and know how to come from point «A» to point «B».
Thirteenth energy endows person with mysteriousness, mysticality. Thanks to depth of this energy its representatives can be keen on esoterics, secret knowledge.
At location of thirteenth energy in plus part of matrix its minuses most often can be related with excessive daring, aggression and desire to destroy and annihilate everything around. If energy is located in minus part of matrix, then its minuses can be related with fear of changes, death or non-confidence in self. Frequent negative manifestation — not to lead matters to end and drop on halfway in connection with constant need for changes.
