ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
Archetype of the ninth energy - sage-hermit. This is a closed energy which loves to go into self, into its "inner cave", where person can be alone with self. From birth people with ninth energy are endowed with light of wisdom, which they can convey to others. To such people characteristic is constant striving to self, to knowledge laid within self.
Ninth arcana likes to study self, surrounding world, and dive into depth of its matter: it's necessary for them to maximally sort out in questions interesting them. Communication with them represents special interest, since they can share unique knowledge and experience, which will help learn something new and expand one's outlook. People with ninth energies well understand essence of people, phenomena, problems and life processes. Ninth energy also manifests in sensitivity and ability of person to subtly feel mood of those around. Can notice vices of other people and know what's worth saying, and what not. However presence of deep wisdom can lead to arrogance, when person with ninth energy starts to look at those around from above down.
Owners of ninth energy feel themselves comfortable in solitude and can even enjoy it for that to more deeply understand their thoughts and feelings. People with this energy are loyal, calm, tactful and respectful. They easily can dive into study of new, trying to dig to very essence. Responsibility and thoroughness are distinctive qualities of ninth energy - if do something, then do qualitatively.
Task of ninth energy - shine to others and convey one's wisdom and life experience. It is important not to remain one on one with one's knowledge, but start to translate them, even if it seems that knowledge is still insufficient.
Main line of minuses of this energy is linked with excessive reservedness, when nines close in their world and don't want to open to people. Moreover, due to one's wisdom, such people can go into negative, self-flagellation and devaluing of one's past achievements. This can lead to feeling of solitude and alienation, what in end can negatively reflect on emotional and psychic well-being of person. Therefore it's important to find balance between solitude and communication with other people, so as not to limit one's potential and not miss possibility to get acquainted with new and interesting people.
Absence of desire to help others can testify to excessive pride, when people with nines consider self above rest. Denial of material can lead to neglect of material world, problems with money and to excessive withdrawal into spirituality.
