ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The eighteenth energy does not have a defined archetype. It is a structureless energy that is associated with the astral body, intuition, sensing. In the classical Tarot, this arcana is called "The Moon," which speaks of attraction, mystery, and the mysticism of this energy. The eighteenth energy is closely related to the subconscious and depth. It is an esoteric energy that attracts everything mysterious, secret, and unknown. It symbolizes the unconscious part of a person and can be expressed in unique abilities for perception and understanding of the surrounding world.
People with the eighteenth energy are mysterious, free in spirit, "flown away" from the real world. They are often in their internal world of thoughts and fantasies, deeply reflective and possess intuitive, sensitive perception. In plus, they find a balance between the internal world and external circumstances, can radiate positivity and optimism. However, in minus, they are prone to depression and apathy.
Representatives of the eighteenth energy possess strong creative potential and are capable of creating their own "magic," which can manifest in various spheres of life, starting from interior design and ending with various professional projects. They can use their creativity and fantasy to transform the mundane into something unusual and magical. Such people can be perceived by those around them as mysterious and mystical personalities, capable of surprising with their non-standard ideas.
This is a closed energy that directs a person to search for their unique path in life, emphasizing the importance of individuality and unusualness. It calls to listen to one's internal "Self" and look for one's own special path in life, not following conventional measures and not meeting standards.
People with this energy are attractive, charismatic, and can themselves attract into their life what they think about. Therefore, it is important to work on one's fears to avoid attracting negative circumstances. They have developed intuition and imagination, which allow them to keenly feel people and events. Thanks to these qualities, they are capable of achieving success in various areas and revealing their talent. Their heightened sensitivity gives them artistry and the ability to learn quickly.
The first direction of minuses is related to excessive sensitivity and closedness. Sometimes they may face addictions, feel like a victim, and fall into depression. The second direction of negative manifestations is fears, which lead to constant doubts and lack of action.
