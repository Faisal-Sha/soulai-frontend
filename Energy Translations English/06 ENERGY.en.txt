ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the sixth energy is the Lovers. This is a structureless, soft, enveloping energy directed at relationships and manifestation of love for oneself, others, a cause, a process. Also, it is the energy of interaction and communication. It represents a complex arcana that can cause difficulties in understanding due to the absence of a clear structure and archetypal form. The energy of this archetype is very sensitive and therefore does not always yield to logical analysis. It is important to open up to intuitive perception to better understand and feel this energy in your life.
People with this energy can show initiative in organizing various events and holidays. They are characterized by a striving for making life brighter and more interesting, they are ready to spend their time and strength to arrange a holiday for themselves and for those around. In plus, such people are hospitable, the doors of their home are always open for friends and loved ones. Due to the fact that the sixth energy feels other people well, its owners often become "psychologists" for those around. They are always open and caring, attentive to problems of other people, ready to listen and understand.
The energy hints at creativity, work with people, openness, passing of love. For such people, beauty around them is important, they love to create a cozy and stylish atmosphere in various spheres of life, be it home, clothing, work or creativity. They can translate their love into matter. This energy is drawn to comfort, it's important for them to feel it around them.
Most of the minus manifestations are related to the excessive sensitivity of this energy. They can be extremely vulnerable, take criticism in their address hard, be touchy. In minus, the sixth energy can manifest in the form of uncertainty in self and a constant feeling of judgment from those around. This can lead to the fact that a person starts to doubt their abilities, becomes selective in communication and often worries because of possible failures. Since people with the sixth energy usually put relationships with people in first place, sometimes they can forget about their own needs and sacrifice their time and resources. Attachment to people and fixation on relationships can lead to the fact that a person completely forgets about their desires, just to please and help the rest. As the sixth energy is related to love and emotions, people with this energy are prone to staying in their dreams and illusions regarding other people. Not seeing the real picture, they make mistakes in their relationships.
Since the energy of the sixth arcana does not possess a clear structure, this can lead to uncertainty in one's decisions and constant doubts in the correctness of the chosen path. Thanks to establishing structure and core, people can gain confidence in themselves.
For owners of the sixth energy, external look is important, but it's necessary to find balance between external and internal. It's important to be open to diversity and accept other people. Avoid idleness and lack of seriousness so as not to allow problems with money and debts.

Energy of the sixth arcana symbolizes all-penetrating love. This is love for oneself, partner, those around, profession, Universe. It reflects selfless and sincere feelings. For a person possessing the sixth energy, it is important to carry out all their actions exclusively from love - learn, search for partner, choose work and much more.
The soul task of the sixth energy is establishing harmonious relationships with the inner "I" and mastering the art of loving oneself, and then the world as a whole, openness and softness. It is important to make all significant decisions exclusively from the state of love. It is necessary to learn to accept and see beauty and perfection in the world. At that, one should accept one's sensitivity and stop idealizing people, understanding that they also have their weaknesses and disadvantages. Idealization and "pink glasses" can create unreal expectations and prevent development and achievement of goals. It is important to see the world as it is, with its advantages and imperfections, and be ready to adapt to changes and life realities.
It is necessary to be open to self-knowledge, understand your strengths and weaknesses, as well as accept yourself such as you are. It is important to overcome indecisiveness, touchiness, doubts, closedness and develop ability to cope with difficulties. You shouldn't ignore your own feelings and interests in striving to get approval of those around. Try to realize and heal emotional wounds related to past grudges and disappointments. Don't fixate on relationships that have outlived themselves and don't bring joy.

Strive for development of your financial literacy to manage money with mind, avoiding wastefulness and making ill-considered financial decisions. Strive for alignment between your inner kindness, sincerity and love, as well as your external look and lifestyle. Develop communicative skills, start many friends, create a holiday around yourself. Your task - develop love, manifest unconditional love for the surrounding world, compassion for all living things, openness and acceptance, which will allow reaching harmony in relationships with self, other people and the world as a whole.
