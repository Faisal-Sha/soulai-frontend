ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the fourth arcana is the Emperor (male energy). It is distinguished by stateliness, calm and global vision. People born with this energy have a certain attitude toward themselves - they love when they are admired and respected, and when their opinion is considered. The archetype of the Emperor, as a rule, does not manifest in a desire to serve someone or something; rather - he prefers to be in the role of a ruler, managing processes and people.
The energy of the Emperor implies stability and steadiness in life, as well as a striving for organization and control over one's life. A person with this energy can show high housekeeping and organization in many aspects of their life, such as household, finances, career, etc. They are capable of making decisions that will provide stability and security for themselves and their family. People with the fourth energy have strategic thinking, they are successful in planning and forecasting, they like to think ahead. Good leaders, managers, owners of their own business can come out of such people. For them, it is important to show care for their personnel, to make the lives of their workers better.
The Emperor possesses great potential for creating and developing a business. He is characterized by high work capacity and a striving for achieving goals. The Emperor is also a symbol of multiplication, management, control and organization, which helps to effectively use one's resources and achieve success.
People with this energy usually possess high logicality, reliability and clarity of thinking, which allows them to make correct and well-thought-out decisions, keep their word and fulfill promises.
Negative manifestations of this energy most often come from two directions:
❖ authority and harshness (the desire to control everything and everyone around can lead to hyper-control, despotism, cruelty, arrogance);
❖ apathy and unwillingness to be an emperor (unwillingness to act and achieve goals, laziness).
