ARCHETYPE
General description of energy
The archetype of the seventh energy is the Warrior (male energy). In classic Tarot, this arcana is called "The Chariot", which symbolizes movement and heralds changes and new opportunities. The seventh energy has a clearly manifested light and dark sides: plus manifestation - movement forward, leadership, constructiveness; negative manifestation - aggression and destruction.
The owner of the seventh energy needs to be in movement, both in physical plan (moving, not sitting in one place, active sport) and in astral-mental plan (movement in career, self-realization, self-knowledge). It is important to have goals and challenges to constantly move through life forward. Thanks to their goal-orientedness and self-sufficiency, owners of the seventh energy can be excellent entrepreneurs, they know how to form a clear plan of actions and follow it. Such people can effectively organize people and gather a team for achievement of certain goals.
In plus manifestation, person with a "seven" can effectively set goals for realization, but if such are absent, then challenges can appear independently in the form of life difficulties, such as problems with health or financial difficulties. People with the seventh energy are self-sufficient, they don't need help from outside and can perform all their tasks independently. However it is important to learn delegation of tasks to avoid minus manifestations related to inability to work in team and attempt to perform all tasks independently.
Seventh energy is a leadership energy, it endows its owners with management qualities, skill to take on leading roles and lead people. People with sevens are responsible, know how to effectively reach set goals, which contributes to their recognition in society and personal realization.
People with the seventh energy don't like to sit in four walls, they prefer activity and movement. They like to travel, engage in sport and any other forms of physical activity, as this activates their energy. Thanks to their flexibility, they easily adapt to new situations and can reach their goals with maximum efficiency.
This energy has three main directions of minuses: warrior-likeness, laziness and/or excessive chaoticness, lack of self-sufficiency. They can be prone to aggression, cruelty and categoricalness, when they consider their opinion the only correct one. "All or nothing" - life position capable of leading to negative consequences and disappointments. For such people it's hard to live in the moment "here and now", as all their thoughts are directed to achievement of goals in future. For people with the seventh energy optimism is important, as they are prone to extremes and can go into minus if something doesn't work out. To preserve positive and flexibility, one should continue moving to new goals and enjoy each day.
